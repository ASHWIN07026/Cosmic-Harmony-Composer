ℹ️ About Cosmic Harmony Composer

🚀 What is This?

Cosmic Harmony Composer is an AI music generator powered by LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) neural networks. It learns cosmic musical patterns and generates original compositions in real-time, entirely on your local machine.

🧠 How It Works

  1. Training: LSTM network learns from cosmic note sequences
  2. Generation: Neural network predicts next note based on context
  3. Export: Generated notes are converted to playable MIDI files

🎼 Understanding the Modes

  • Structured: Constrained to musical scales, harmonic and pleasant
  • Random: Pure probability-based, unpredictable and chaotic
  • Experimental: Fusion of both, surreal and unusual

🎹 Instruments

Choose from 8 different MIDI instruments. Each brings a unique character to your composition:

  • 🎹 Piano - Classical warmth
  • 🎻 Strings - Symphonic elegance
  • 🪈 Flute - Bright and airy
  • 🔧 Synth - Electronic cosmic sounds
  • 🔔 Bells - Mystical chimes
  • 🥁 Marimba - Percussive resonance
  • 🎼 Organ - Deep atmospheric
  • 🎵 Harp - Delicate plucking

🎼 Musical Scales

Each scale offers a different mood and character:

  • Major Scales: Happy, bright, optimistic
  • Minor Scales: Melancholic, dark, introspective
  • Pentatonic: Asian, Eastern, exotic
  • Blues: Soulful, emotional, expressive
  • Chromatic: Experimental, all notes

🔧 Technical Details

Framework: Flask + TensorFlow/Keras

Model: 2-layer LSTM (128 units each)

Training: 5 epochs on cosmic note corpus

Export: MIDI format (music21 library)

Completely Free: No API keys, no paid services

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Try different combinations of instrument + scale + mode
  • Structured mode works best for listenable music
  • Experiment mode creates surreal, unexpected compositions
  • Slower tempos (60-90 BPM) sound more contemplative
  • Faster tempos (150+ BPM) create energetic pieces
  • Minor scales + slow tempo = cinematic/emotional
  • Major scales + fast tempo = uplifting/energetic

🎵 Playing Your Creations

Downloaded MIDI files can be played with:

  • Windows Media Player (built-in)
  • Audacity (free, can convert to MP3)
  • MuseScore (free, see sheet music)
  • Any DAW (Ableton, FL Studio, etc.)
